THE FINAL CHAPTER

A Letter To You ♡

Dear you,

Sometimes I think about how funny it is that we met all the way back in 2018 and I never imagined that you would still be part of my life years later.

I was literally scared of you. 😂 I tried to avoid you, you were asking for my student card, and somehow we went from all of that to dancing together and you asking me to teach you how to twerk. 😭🤣

We hardly see each other. You disappear. Then you come back like you never left. And somehow, our friendship just continues exactly where we left it.

And then this year we went on our first international trip together. Mozambique. 🇲🇿

From meeting in varsity in 2018 to crossing a border together in 2026... sometimes life creates the most unexpected stories.

I'm grateful for every laugh, every ridiculous moment, every random conversation and every memory we've made along the way.

I don't know what the next few years will look like, or how many more places we'll visit, but I hope we keep adding memories to this little story of ours.

And when you disappear again... 😂 just know I'll probably still be here when you randomly return.

Thank you for being part of my story. I'm really glad our paths crossed.

With love,
Your favourite troublemaker 💕

♡ ✨ ♡

This isn't the end of our story.

It's just another page.
